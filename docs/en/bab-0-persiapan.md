# Chapter 0: Preparation

Before memorizing a single word, let's get to know the "engine" of Mandarin: its script, how its sounds are written (pinyin), and its tones. Think of it like learning programming: this is installing + setting up the environment before writing your first line of code.

No vocabulary to memorize in this chapter. There's only one goal: you understand pinyin, can tell the four tones apart, and know why 不 is sometimes read *bú*. Once this foundation is solid, every chapter after this gets way easier.

This chapter is split into two pages:

- [Pinyin & Tones](/en/bab-0-pinyin-nada) — §§0.1–0.5: what Mandarin is, simplified vs traditional, pinyin, the four tones, and tone sandhi.
- [Hands-on Practice](/en/bab-0-praktik) — §§0.6–0.8: installing a pinyin keyboard, study tips, daily tone drill, plus exercises.
