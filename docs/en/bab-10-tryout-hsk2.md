# Chapter 10: HSK 2 Mock Exam

A 24-question mock exam to measure your readiness before taking the real HSK 2. Take it seriously, do not peek at the vocabulary lists, and record your score at the end.

## What the real exam looks like

The real HSK 2 has **70 questions**: 35 listening, 25 reading, 10 writing. Total **200 points**, and you pass with **120**.

This web version is an adaptation: **24 questions, no audio**. The listening part is converted into reading questions, since what is tested stays the same: sentence and vocabulary comprehension. To be honest, this is not a replacement for the real exam, but it is enough to tell whether you are ready or not.

## How to score yourself

Out of 24 questions, **20 count toward your score** (Parts A through D). The four warm-up questions are unscored.

Your score formula: **correct ÷ 20 × 200**. Example: 15 correct means 15 / 20 x 200 = 150. The real exam's passing mark is **120**, which means at least **12 correct answers**.

## Tips

- Set aside about 30 uninterrupted minutes.
- Work in order, from the warm-up through Part D.
- Part D (word ordering) is closest to the real writing section, do not skip it.
