# Chapter 11 · 11.9–11.12: Things & Places

This page collects vocabulary for the things around you: from the kitchen, clothes, the city, to nature and seasons. All words here are HSK 3, so think of them as upgrades from Chapters 4 and 7.

## 11.9 Home & kitchen (冰箱, 空调, 灯, 菜单, 面包, 蛋糕, 盘子, 瓶子, 筷子, 碗, 饱, 饿, 渴, 甜, 饮料, 啤酒, 米, 香蕉)

| Hanzi | Pinyin | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 冰箱 | bīngxiāng | refrigerator |
| 空调 | kōngtiáo | air conditioner |
| 灯 | dēng | lamp |
| 菜单 | càidān | menu |
| 面包 | miànbāo | bread |
| 蛋糕 | dàngāo | cake |
| 盘子 | pánzi | plate |
| 瓶子 | píngzi | bottle |
| 筷子 | kuàizi | chopsticks |
| 碗 | wǎn | bowl |
| 饱 | bǎo | full (stomach) |
| 饿 | è | hungry |
| 渴 | kě | thirsty |
| 甜 | tián | sweet |
| 饮料 | yǐnliào | drink; beverage |
| 啤酒 | píjiǔ | beer |
| 米 | mǐ | rice |
| 香蕉 | xiāngjiāo | banana |
| 新鲜 | xīnxiān | fresh |

<div class="contoh"><div class="hz">我饿了，想吃面包。</div><div class="py">Wǒ è le, xiǎng chī miànbāo.</div><div class="id">I'm hungry, I want to eat bread.</div></div>

<div class="contoh"><div class="hz">菜单上有蛋糕吗？</div><div class="py">Càidān shang yǒu dàngāo ma?</div><div class="id">Is there cake on the menu?</div></div>

<div class="contoh"><div class="hz">我渴了，想喝饮料。</div><div class="py">Wǒ kě le, xiǎng hē yǐnliào.</div><div class="id">I'm thirsty, I want something to drink.</div></div>

<div class="bedah"><strong>冰箱</strong> (bīngxiāng, refrigerator) = 冰 (bīng, ice) + 箱 (xiāng, box). Literally "ice box".</div>

<div class="bedah"><strong>空调</strong> (kōngtiáo, air conditioner) = 空 (kōng, air) + 调 (tiáo, to regulate). So it means "air regulator". Logical.</div>

> 💡 The stomach trio: 我饿了 (I'm hungry), 我渴了 (I'm thirsty), 我饱了 (I'm full). The pattern is always 我 + adjective + 了.

<div class="contoh"><div class="hz">这件衬衫很漂亮。</div><div class="py">Zhè jiàn chènshān hěn piàoliang.</div><div class="id">This shirt is really nice.</div></div>

<div class="contoh"><div class="hz">他的皮鞋是新的。</div><div class="py">Tā de píxié shì xīn de.</div><div class="id">His leather shoes are new.</div></div>

<div class="bedah"><strong>照相机</strong> (zhàoxiàngjī, camera) = 照 (zhào, to photograph) + 相 (xiàng, appearance) + 机 (jī, machine). An "appearance-capturing machine", cool.</div>

<div class="bedah"><strong>信用卡</strong> (xìnyòngkǎ, credit card) = 信用 (xìnyòng, trust/credit) + 卡 (kǎ, card). Credit is at its core the bank's trust in you.</div>

Note: 辆, 条, 种 are measure words. 辆 for vehicles (一辆车), 条 for long things (一条鱼), 种 for kinds (一种水果).

<div class="contoh"><div class="hz">超市在银行附近。</div><div class="py">Chāoshì zài yínháng fùjìn.</div><div class="id">The supermarket is near the bank.</div></div>

<div class="contoh"><div class="hz">我每天坐地铁去上班。</div><div class="py">Wǒ měitiān zuò dìtiě qù shàngbān.</div><div class="id">I take the subway to work every day.</div></div>

<div class="contoh"><div class="hz">这个城市有很多公园。</div><div class="py">Zhège chéngshì yǒu hěn duō gōngyuán.</div><div class="id">This city has lots of parks.</div></div>

<div class="bedah"><strong>地铁</strong> (dìtiě, subway) = 地 (dì, ground) + 铁 (tiě, iron). An iron train running under the ground. Fits.</div>

<div class="bedah"><strong>银行</strong> (yínháng, bank) = 银 (yín, silver) + 行 (háng, once meant "trading firm"). Historical trace: early banks were silver-exchange shops.</div>

<div class="bedah"><strong>电梯</strong> (diàntī, elevator) = 电 (diàn, electricity) + 梯 (tī, ladder). "Electric ladder", easy to remember.</div>

> 💡 附近 (fùjìn) means "nearby", so the pattern is always: place + 在 + other place + 附近.

<div class="contoh"><div class="hz">春天有很多花。</div><div class="py">Chūntiān yǒu hěn duō huā.</div><div class="id">There are lots of flowers in spring.</div></div>

<div class="contoh"><div class="hz">冬天很冷，夏天很热。</div><div class="py">Dōngtiān hěn lěng, xiàtiān hěn rè.</div><div class="id">Winter is very cold, summer is very hot.</div></div>

<div class="bedah"><strong>熊猫</strong> (xióngmāo, panda) = 熊 (xióng, bear) + 猫 (māo, cat). "Bear cat". The most honest name in the world.</div>

The four directions: 东 (east), 南 (south), 西 (west), 北方 (north). The four seasons: 春夏秋冬. Season + 天 pattern: 春天, 夏天, 秋天, 冬天.
