# Chapter 11 · 11.5–11.8: Activities & Work

This page focuses on everyday activities up to the world of work: from cleaning the house and brushing your teeth, to office meetings and weekend sports. All the words below are HSK 3 vocabulary.

<div class="contoh"><div class="hz">我明天有一个会议。</div><div class="py">Wǒ míngtiān yǒu yī gè huìyì.</div><div class="id">I have a meeting tomorrow.</div></div>

<div class="contoh"><div class="hz">她今天迟到了。</div><div class="py">Tā jīntiān chídào le.</div><div class="id">She is late today.</div></div>

<div class="contoh"><div class="hz">我决定请假一天。</div><div class="py">Wǒ juédìng qǐngjià yī tiān.</div><div class="id">I decided to take one day off.</div></div>

<div class="bedah"><strong>办公室</strong> (bàngōngshì, office) = 办 (bàn, to handle) + 公 (gōng, public) + 室 (shì, room). An office is a room for handling public affairs.</div>

<div class="bedah"><strong>迟到</strong> (chídào, to be late) = 迟 (chí, slow) + 到 (dào, to arrive). To arrive slowly = to be late. A very neat pattern.</div>

<div class="bedah"><strong>决定</strong> (juédìng, to decide) = 决 (jué, to decide) + 定 (dìng, to fix/settle). Both parts are about being firm, so the meaning is strong: to truly make a decision.</div>

<div class="bedah"><strong>搬</strong> (bān, to move things) = 扌 (hand) + 般 (bān, sound hint). Moving house is done with your hands, hence the hand radical.</div>

<div class="contoh"><div class="hz">我要努力完成作业。</div><div class="py">Wǒ yào nǔlì wánchéng zuòyè.</div><div class="id">I have to work hard to finish my homework.</div></div>

<div class="bedah"><strong>复习</strong> (fùxí, to review a lesson) = 复 (fù, again/back) + 习 (xí, to study/practice). To study again = to review.</div>

<div class="bedah"><strong>中文</strong> (Zhōngwén, Chinese language) = 中 (zhōng, middle) + 文 (wén, writing/language). The language of the "middle kingdom" (中国).</div>

<div class="bedah"><strong>借</strong> (jiè, to borrow) = 亻 (person) + 昔 (xī, sound hint). Honest note: the sound hint is loose (xī vs jiè), but the pattern is clear: borrowing is something people do with each other.</div>

## 11.8 Sports & entertainment (锻炼, 比赛, 体育, 爬山, 骑, 音乐, 游戏, 节目, 节日, 周末, 故事, etc.)

| 音乐 | yīnyuè | music |
| 游戏 | yóuxì | game |
| 节目 | jiémù | show; program |
| 节日 | jiérì | festival |
| 周末 | zhōumò | weekend |
| 故事 | gùshi | story |

<div class="contoh"><div class="hz">周末我们去爬山。</div><div class="py">Zhōumò wǒmen qù páshān.</div><div class="id">We go mountain climbing on the weekend.</div></div>

<div class="contoh"><div class="hz">我喜欢听音乐，看节目。</div><div class="py">Wǒ xǐhuan tīng yīnyuè, kàn jiémù.</div><div class="id">I like listening to music and watching shows.</div></div>

<div class="bedah"><strong>锻炼</strong> (duànliàn, to exercise) = 锻 (duàn, to forge) + 炼 (liàn, to smelt). Honest note: the "exercise" meaning is an extension from the blacksmith's world (forging and smelting metal = training), not something you can guess directly from the components. Memorize the pattern instead of guessing.</div>
