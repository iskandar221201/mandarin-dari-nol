# Chapter 11: HSK 3 Vocabulary

Welcome to HSK 3. At this level you add **300 new words**, bringing the total to **600**. The difference from HSK 2: words get more abstract (concepts, feelings, function words) and many verbs carry context-dependent meanings.

This chapter is split into 4 pages for easy digestion:

| Page | Theme | Contents |
|---|---|---|
| [11.1–11.4: People & Society](/en/bab-11-manusia-masyarakat) | People & relations | Extended family, body & health, feelings & traits, society |
| [11.5–11.8: Activities & Work](/en/bab-11-kegiatan-kerja) | Activities | Work & office, studying, daily activities, sports & fun |
| [11.9–11.12: Things & Places](/en/bab-11-benda-tempat) | Objects & locations | Home & kitchen, clothes & items, city & places, nature & directions |
| [11.13–11.16: Concepts & Function Words](/en/bab-11-konsep-fungsi) | Abstract & function | Abstract concepts, conjunctions, adverbials, function words |

## How to use

1. Read one page per session, not all at once.
2. Every word comes with Hanzi + Pinyin + meaning. Example sentences show real usage.
3. Component breakdowns help memorization, but don't force them when the pattern is unclear.
4. Do the quizzes on each page before moving on.
