# Chapter 11 · 11.1–11.4: People & Society

HSK 3 adds 300 new words, bringing the total to 600. This page focuses on people and relationships: extended family, body and health, feelings and traits, and the society around you.

## 11.1 Extended family

HSK 2 introduced 爸爸妈妈 through 丈夫妻子. HSK 3 adds the grandparent generation and uncles/aunts:

| Hanzi | Pinyin | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 阿姨 | āyí | aunt (also a friendly address for older women) |
| 奶奶 | nǎinai | grandma (paternal) |
| 爷爷 | yéye | grandpa (paternal) |
| 叔叔 | shūshu | uncle |
| 结婚 | jiéhūn | to marry |
| 张 | zhāng | the surname Zhang |

<div class="contoh"><div class="hz">我奶奶今年八十岁。</div><div class="py">Wǒ nǎinai jīnnián bāshí suì.</div><div class="id">My grandma is 80 years old this year.</div></div>

<div class="contoh"><div class="hz">我叔叔结婚了。</div><div class="py">Wǒ shūshu jiéhūn le.</div><div class="id">My uncle got married.</div></div>

<div class="bedah"><strong>婚</strong> (hūn, to marry) = 女 (woman) + 昏 (hūn, dusk). Sound pattern: 昏 lends the hūn sound.</div>

Note: 奶奶 and 爷爷 are specifically the paternal grandparents.

## 11.3 Feelings & traits

Feelings, traits, and the mental verbs that bring conversations to life:

| Hanzi | Pinyin | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 担心 | dānxīn | to worry |
| 放心 | fàngxīn | to feel relieved |
| 害怕 | hàipà | to fear |
| 生气 | shēngqì | angry |
| 难过 | nánguò | sad |
| 聪明 | cōngming | smart |
| 热情 | rèqíng | warm, enthusiastic |
| 奇怪 | qíguài | strange |
| 可爱 | kě'ài | cute |
| 年轻 | niánqīng | young |
| 感兴趣 | gǎnxìngqù | to be interested in |
| 满意 | mǎnyì | satisfied |
| 明白 | míngbai | to get it, understand clearly |
| 了解 | liǎojiě | to understand in depth |
| 记得 | jìde | to remember |
| 忘记 | wàngjì | to forget |
| 认为 | rènwéi | to think, to be of the opinion |
| 认真 | rènzhēn | serious, diligent |
| 相信 | xiāngxìn | to believe |
| 着急 | zháojí | anxious |
| 愿意 | yuànyì | willing |
| 同意 | tóngyì | to agree |
| 发现 | fāxiàn | to discover, to realize |
| 注意 | zhùyì | to pay attention |
| 小心 | xiǎoxīn | careful |
| 爱好 | àihào | hobby |

<div class="contoh"><div class="hz">别担心，我很好。</div><div class="py">Bié dānxīn, wǒ hěn hǎo.</div><div class="id">Don't worry, I'm fine.</div></div>

<div class="contoh"><div class="hz">他生气了，因为我忘记了。</div><div class="py">Tā shēngqì le, yīnwèi wǒ wàngjì le.</div><div class="id">He got angry because I forgot.</div></div>

<div class="contoh"><div class="hz">我对中文很感兴趣。</div><div class="py">Wǒ duì Zhōngwén hěn gǎnxìngqù.</div><div class="id">I'm really interested in Chinese.</div></div>

<div class="bedah"><strong>忘</strong> (wàng, to forget) = 亡 (wáng, to perish, to disappear) + 心 (heart). "A heart that goes missing" = to forget. A clean meaning pattern.</div>

<div class="bedah"><strong>怕</strong> (pà, to fear) = 忄 (heart, feeling) + 白 (bái, white). Scared until your face turns white. Meaning pattern.</div>

<div class="bedah"><strong>认</strong> (rèn, to recognize) = 讠 (speech) + 人 (person). "A person's words" = to recognize, to acknowledge. Meaning pattern.</div>

<div class="bedah"><strong>聪</strong> (cōng, smart) = 耳 (ear) on the left marks this as hearing-related. Sharp ears were seen as a sign of intelligence. Meaning pattern.</div>

<div class="bedah"><strong>意</strong> (yì, meaning, intention) = 音 (yīn, sound) + 心 (heart). "The sound of the heart" = intention. Meaning pattern.</div>

Note: 担心 vs 放心 are opposites, to worry vs to feel relieved. 了解 vs 明白 both mean "to understand", but 了解 is understanding through getting to know something deeply, 明白 is getting it clearly. 记得 vs 忘记 are another opposite pair, to remember vs to forget.

## 11.4 Society & relationships

The people around you: neighbors, coworkers, guests, and everyone in between:

| Hanzi | Pinyin | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 邻居 | línjū | neighbor |
| 同事 | tóngshì | colleague |
| 客人 | kèrén | guest |
| 关系 | guānxì | relationship |
| 关心 | guānxīn | to care about |
| 照顾 | zhàogù | to take care of |
| 聊天 | liáotiān | to chat |
| 见面 | jiànmiàn | to meet up |
| 经理 | jīnglǐ | manager |
| 校长 | xiàozhǎng | school principal |
| 司机 | sījī | driver |
| 欢迎 | huānyíng | welcome |
| 位 | wèi | polite measure word for people |
| 有名 | yǒumíng | famous |
| 遇到 | yùdào | to run into, to experience |
| 习惯 | xíguàn | habit |
| 别人 | biéren | other people |
| 自己 | zìjǐ | oneself |

<div class="contoh"><div class="hz">我的邻居很热情。</div><div class="py">Wǒ de línjū hěn rèqíng.</div><div class="id">My neighbor is very warm and friendly.</div></div>

<div class="contoh"><div class="hz">我们昨天见面了。</div><div class="py">Wǒmen zuótiān jiànmiàn le.</div><div class="id">We met up yesterday.</div></div>

<div class="contoh"><div class="hz">谢谢你的关心！</div><div class="py">Xièxie nǐ de guānxīn!</div><div class="id">Thanks for caring!</div></div>

<div class="bedah"><strong>照</strong> (zhào, in 照顾 = to take care of) = 日 (sun) + 召 (zhào). Sound pattern: 召 lends the zhào sound.</div>

<div class="bedah"><strong>客</strong> (kè, guest) = 宀 (roof) + 各 (gè). Sound pattern: 各 lends a similar sound.</div>

Note: 位 is the polite measure word for people, e.g. 一位客人 (one guest). 关心 vs 照顾, 关心 = caring in your heart, 照顾 = caring through actions. 自己 vs 别人, yourself vs other people.
