# Chapter 12 · 12.6–12.13: 把, 被 & Advanced Comparison

In §8.11 you met 把. Now the full version, plus its twin: 被 (passive).

## 12.6 Full 把

Pattern: **把 + object + V + complement** = handle the object until there's a result.

When 把 is **required**:
1. The object is definite (not "a book", but "the book").
2. There's a result/directional complement after the verb (关上, 写完, 干净).
3. The action changes or moves the object.

<div class="contoh"><div class="hz">把书放在桌子上。</div><div class="py">Bǎ shū fàng zài zhuōzi shang.</div><div class="id">Put the book on the table.</div></div>

<div class="contoh"><div class="hz">把房间打扫干净。</div><div class="py">Bǎ fángjiān dǎsǎo gānjìng.</div><div class="id">Clean the room until it's spotless.</div></div>

<div class="contoh"><div class="hz">把这件事告诉他。</div><div class="py">Bǎ zhè jiàn shì gàosu tā.</div><div class="id">Tell him about this matter.</div></div>

When 把 is **forbidden**:
- Verbs that don't change the object: 喜欢, 认识, 知道 (wrong: 把他喜欢).
- No complement: 把书拿 (incomplete, needs 把书拿走/拿来).
- Indefinite object: 把一本书放在… (odd, use 拿一本书放在…).

## 12.7 被: the passive

Pattern: **(Object) + 被 + (doer) + V + complement/了** = …-ed by …

<div class="contoh"><div class="hz">我的书被他拿走了。</div><div class="py">Wǒ de shū bèi tā ná zǒu le.</div><div class="id">My book was taken by him.</div></div>

<div class="contoh"><div class="hz">电视被弟弟打开了。</div><div class="py">Diànshì bèi dìdi dǎkāi le.</div><div class="id">The TV was turned on by little brother.</div></div>

<div class="contoh"><div class="hz">我的车被他开走了，现在怎么办？</div><div class="py">Wǒ de chē bèi tā kāi zǒu le, xiànzài zěnme bàn?</div><div class="id">My car was driven away by him, what now?</div></div>

Trap: 被 often carries a negative tone (accidents, losses), but not always. The doer can be dropped: 书被拿走了.

## 12.8 Contrast: 把 vs 被

The same sentence, two viewpoints:

| Active (把) | Passive (被) |
|---|---|
| 我把作业写完了。 | 作业被我写完了。 |
| 他把门关上了。 | 门被他关上了。 |

<div class="contoh"><div class="hz">我把作业写完了，所以作业被我写完了。</div><div class="py">Wǒ bǎ zuòyè xiě wán le, suǒyǐ zuòyè bèi wǒ xiě wán le.</div><div class="id">I finished my homework, so my homework was finished by me.</div></div>

The point: 把 spotlights the doer acting; 被 spotlights the object affected. Choose based on what you want to emphasize.

## 12.9 越来越 + Adj

Pattern: **越来越 + adjective** = increasingly … (change over time).

<div class="contoh"><div class="hz">天气越来越冷了，多穿一点儿。</div><div class="py">Tiānqì yuèláiyuè lěng le, duō chuān yìdiǎnr.</div><div class="id">The weather is getting colder, wear more.</div></div>

<div class="contoh"><div class="hz">他越来越高了，快一米八了。</div><div class="py">Tā yuèláiyuè gāo le, kuài yì mǐ bā le.</div><div class="id">He's getting taller, almost 1.8 meters.</div></div>

<div class="contoh"><div class="hz">我的中文越来越好了。</div><div class="py">Wǒ de zhōngwén yuèláiyuè hǎo le.</div><div class="id">My Mandarin is getting better and better.</div></div>

## 12.10 越 + V + 越 + Adj

Pattern: **越 + verb + 越 + adjective** = the more (you do X), the more (Y).

<div class="contoh"><div class="hz">他越想越生气。</div><div class="py">Tā yuè xiǎng yuè shēngqì.</div><div class="id">The more he thinks about it, the angrier he gets.</div></div>

<div class="contoh"><div class="hz">中文越学越难，但是越学越有意思。</div><div class="py">Zhōngwén yuè xué yuè nán, dànshì yuè xué yuè yǒu yìsi.</div><div class="id">The more you study Mandarin the harder it gets, but the more interesting too.</div></div>

Trap: the two 越 must be parallel (越V越Adj). Don't mix with 越来越 (which has no verb in the middle).

## 12.11 跟…一样 + Adj

Pattern: **A + 跟 + B + 一样 + adjective** = A is as … as B.

<div class="contoh"><div class="hz">你跟他一样高。</div><div class="py">Nǐ gēn tā yíyàng gāo.</div><div class="id">You're as tall as him.</div></div>

<div class="contoh"><div class="hz">我的手机跟你的一样。</div><div class="py">Wǒ de shǒujī gēn nǐ de yíyàng.</div><div class="id">My phone is the same as yours.</div></div>

<div class="contoh"><div class="hz">这家餐厅跟那家一样贵。</div><div class="py">Zhè jiā cāntīng gēn nà jiā yíyàng guì.</div><div class="id">This restaurant is as expensive as that one.</div></div>

Note: 跟 here means "with" (comparison), not "follow". 一样 can stand alone: 我们一样大.

## 12.12 比…更/还 + Adj

Pattern: **A + 比 + B + 更/还 + adjective** = A is *even more* … than B (strengthened 比).

<div class="contoh"><div class="hz">他比我哥哥还高。</div><div class="py">Tā bǐ wǒ gēge hái gāo.</div><div class="id">He's even taller than my older brother.</div></div>

<div class="contoh"><div class="hz">今天比昨天更冷。</div><div class="py">Jīntiān bǐ zuótiān gèng lěng.</div><div class="id">Today is even colder than yesterday.</div></div>

Trap: 更 = more (neutral), 还 = even (amazed tone). Both strengthen 比, never replace it.

## 12.13 A + 没(有) + B + 这么/那么 + Adj

The formal version of the HSK 2 pattern: **A + 没有 + B + 这么/那么 + adjective** = A is not as … as B.

<div class="contoh"><div class="hz">你没有我胖，别担心。</div><div class="py">Nǐ méiyǒu wǒ pàng, bié dānxīn.</div><div class="id">You're not as fat as me, don't worry.</div></div>

<div class="contoh"><div class="hz">他没有你那么忙。</div><div class="py">Tā méiyǒu nǐ nàme máng.</div><div class="id">He's not as busy as you.</div></div>

Trap: 这么 (near/speaker) vs 那么 (far). 没 can replace 没有 here.
