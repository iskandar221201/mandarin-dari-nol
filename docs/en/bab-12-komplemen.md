# Chapter 12 · 12.1–12.5: Complements

A complement is a small tail attached behind a verb or adjective to explain its **result** or **direction**. This is the biggest HSK 3 topic and the one that most changes how you speak: without complements, Mandarin sounds "unfinished".

## 12.1 Directional complements: V + 来/去

Pattern: **V + 来/去** = action + direction relative to the speaker.

| Pair | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| 上来 / 上去 | come up / go up | 他上来了 |
| 下来 / 下去 | come down / go down | 你先下去 |
| 进来 / 进去 | come in / go in | 请进来 |
| 出来 / 出去 | come out / go out | 他跑出去了 |
| 回来 / 回去 | come back / go back | 我回来了 |
| 起来 | get up / start | 站起来！ |

<div class="contoh"><div class="hz">请进来，外面很冷。</div><div class="py">Qǐng jìnlái, wàimiàn hěn lěng.</div><div class="id">Please come in, it's very cold outside.</div></div>

<div class="contoh"><div class="hz">他跑出去了，你快去找他。</div><div class="py">Tā pǎo chūqù le, nǐ kuài qù zhǎo tā.</div><div class="id">He ran out, go find him quickly.</div></div>

<div class="contoh"><div class="hz">我回来了！你吃饭了吗？</div><div class="py">Wǒ huílái le! Nǐ chī fàn le ma?</div><div class="id">I'm back! Have you eaten?</div></div>

<div class="contoh"><div class="hz">站起来，拿起你的书。</div><div class="py">Zhàn qǐlái, ná qǐ nǐ de shū.</div><div class="id">Stand up, pick up your book.</div></div>

Traps:
- 来 = toward the speaker, 去 = away from the speaker. "他上来了" (he came up here, to me) vs "他上去了" (he went up there).
- 起来 has a figurative "start" sense: 笑起来 (laugh → start laughing), 想起来 (recall).

## 12.2 Result complements: V + 好/完/到/见/住

Pattern: **V + result complement** = a finished action with a visible result.

| Complement | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| 完 | finished | 吃完, 写完 |
| 好 | done/ready | 做好, 准备好 |
| 到 | reach/obtain | 看到, 找到 |
| 见 | perceived | 听见, 看见 |
| 住 | held | 记住 |

<div class="contoh"><div class="hz">我吃完了，我们走吧。</div><div class="py">Wǒ chī wán le, wǒmen zǒu ba.</div><div class="id">I've finished eating, let's go.</div></div>

<div class="contoh"><div class="hz">你的作业写完了吗？</div><div class="py">Nǐ de zuòyè xiě wán le ma?</div><div class="id">Have you finished writing your homework?</div></div>

<div class="contoh"><div class="hz">我看到他了，他在公园里。</div><div class="py">Wǒ kàn dào tā le, tā zài gōngyuán lǐ.</div><div class="id">I saw him, he's in the park.</div></div>

<div class="contoh"><div class="hz">你听见了吗？外面有人唱歌。</div><div class="py">Nǐ tīngjiàn le ma? Wàimiàn yǒu rén chànggē.</div><div class="id">Did you hear? Someone is singing outside.</div></div>

Trap: 了 goes **after** the complement (吃完了, not 吃了完). The result complement can't be separated from its verb.

## 12.3 Potential: V + 得/不 + complement

Pattern: **V + 得 + complement** (can) / **V + 不 + complement** (can't) = able or not able to reach the result.

<div class="contoh"><div class="hz">这个字太小，我看不见。</div><div class="py">Zhè ge zì tài xiǎo, wǒ kàn bu jiàn.</div><div class="id">This character is too small, I can't see it.</div></div>

<div class="contoh"><div class="hz">今天的作业太多，我做不完。</div><div class="py">Jīntiān de zuòyè tài duō, wǒ zuò bu wán.</div><div class="id">Today's homework is too much, I can't finish it.</div></div>

<div class="contoh"><div class="hz">门太小，车进不去。</div><div class="py">Mén tài xiǎo, chē jìn bu qù.</div><div class="id">The door is too small, the car can't get in.</div></div>

<div class="contoh"><div class="hz">你听得清楚吗？请再说一遍。</div><div class="py">Nǐ tīng de qīngchu ma? Qǐng zài shuō yí biàn.</div><div class="id">Can you hear clearly? Please say it once more.</div></div>

Traps:
- 得 here is read with a neutral tone **de**, not děi (must). Don't mix them up.
- Contrast with §12.4: V+得+complement (can/can't) vs V+得+adjective (manner).

## 12.4 State complement: V + 得 + Adj

Pattern: **V + 得 + adjective** = doing something *in what manner*.

<div class="contoh"><div class="hz">他跑得很快，每天都锻炼。</div><div class="py">Tā pǎo de hěn kuài, měi tiān dōu duànliàn.</div><div class="id">He runs fast, he exercises every day.</div></div>

<div class="contoh"><div class="hz">她说得很好，大家都喜欢听。</div><div class="py">Tā shuō de hěn hǎo, dàjiā dōu xǐhuan tīng.</div><div class="id">She speaks well, everyone likes listening.</div></div>

<div class="contoh"><div class="hz">你写得很清楚，老师很高兴。</div><div class="py">Nǐ xiě de hěn qīngchu, lǎoshī hěn gāoxìng.</div><div class="id">You write very clearly, the teacher is delighted.</div></div>

The homophone trio (same sound, different jobs):
- 的 (de): possession/description, e.g. 我的书
- 地 (de): manner adverbial, e.g. 慢慢地走
- 得 (de): complement, e.g. 跑得快

## 12.5 Adjective + 极了

Pattern: **Adj + 极了** = extremely … (maximum emphasis, no 很).

<div class="contoh"><div class="hz">好极了！我们明天去爬山吧。</div><div class="py">Hǎo jí le! Wǒmen míngtiān qù páshān ba.</div><div class="id">Wonderful! Let's go mountain climbing tomorrow.</div></div>

<div class="contoh"><div class="hz">这件衣服漂亮极了，我很喜欢。</div><div class="py">Zhè jiàn yīfu piàoliang jí le, wǒ hěn xǐhuan.</div><div class="id">This dress is gorgeous, I love it.</div></div>

<div class="contoh"><div class="hz">今天忙极了，一共开了三个会。</div><div class="py">Jīntiān máng jí le, yígòng kāi le sān ge huì.</div><div class="id">Today was insanely busy, three meetings in total.</div></div>

Trap: 极了 never takes 很 (wrong: 很好极了). It's like 非常 but more expressive and casual.
