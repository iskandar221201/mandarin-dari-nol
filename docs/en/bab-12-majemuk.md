# Chapter 12 · 12.14–12.21: Compound Sentences

HSK 3 is the reasoning level, connecting two clauses with paired conjunctions. The patterns are always **paired**, memorize the pair, not just the words.

## 12.14 不但…而且…

Pattern: **不但 A, 而且 B** = not only A, but also B (positive, strengthening tone).

<div class="contoh"><div class="hz">他不但聪明，而且很努力。</div><div class="py">Tā búdàn cōngming, érqiě hěn nǔlì.</div><div class="id">He's not only smart, but also hardworking.</div></div>

<div class="contoh"><div class="hz">这个菜不但好吃，而且很便宜。</div><div class="py">Zhè ge cài búdàn hǎochī, érqiě hěn piányi.</div><div class="id">This dish is not only tasty, but also cheap.</div></div>

Trap: 而且 can't be used for negative things ("不但贵，而且难吃" sounds odd). For negatives use 还: 又贵又难吃.

## 12.15 如果…就…

Pattern: **如果 A, (就) B** = if A, then B (neutral condition).

<div class="contoh"><div class="hz">如果下雨，我们就不去了。</div><div class="py">Rúguǒ xià yǔ, wǒmen jiù bu qù le.</div><div class="id">If it rains, we won't go.</div></div>

<div class="contoh"><div class="hz">如果你有问题，就问我吧。</div><div class="py">Rúguǒ nǐ yǒu wèntí, jiù wèn wǒ ba.</div><div class="id">If you have questions, just ask me.</div></div>

Note: 就 can be dropped, but keeping it stresses the consequence.

## 12.16 只要…就…

Pattern: **只要 A, 就 B** = as long as A, then B (a *sufficient* condition, easy to meet).

<div class="contoh"><div class="hz">只要你来，我们就很开心。</div><div class="py">Zhǐyào nǐ lái, wǒmen jiù hěn kāixīn.</div><div class="id">As long as you come, we're happy.</div></div>

<div class="contoh"><div class="hz">只要你练习，中文就会越来越好。</div><div class="py">Zhǐyào nǐ liànxí, zhōngwén jiù huì yuèláiyuè hǎo.</div><div class="id">As long as you practice, your Mandarin will keep improving.</div></div>

只要 vs 如果: 只要 = a light condition ("as long as you're willing"), 如果 = a neutral condition ("if").

## 12.17 只有…才…

Pattern: **只有 A, 才 B** = only if A, then B (a *necessary* condition, must be met).

<div class="contoh"><div class="hz">只有你来，我才去。</div><div class="py">Zhǐyǒu nǐ lái, wǒ cái qù.</div><div class="id">Only if you come will I go.</div></div>

<div class="contoh"><div class="hz">只有你帮忙，我才能完成工作。</div><div class="py">Zhǐyǒu nǐ bāngmáng, wǒ cái néng wánchéng gōngzuò.</div><div class="id">Only with your help can I finish the work.</div></div>

The condition trio:
- 如果 = if (neutral)
- 只要 = as long as (light condition)
- 只有…才 = only if (mandatory condition)

## 12.18 除了…(以外)…都/也…

Pattern: **除了 X (以外), …都/也…** = besides X, (also)…

<div class="contoh"><div class="hz">除了他，我们都去过北京。</div><div class="py">Chúle tā, wǒmen dōu qù guo Běijīng.</div><div class="id">Besides him, we've all been to Beijing.</div></div>

<div class="contoh"><div class="hz">除了中文以外，他还会说英文。</div><div class="py">Chúle zhōngwén yǐwài, tā hái huì shuō yīngwén.</div><div class="id">Besides Mandarin, he can also speak English.</div></div>

<div class="contoh"><div class="hz">除了星期天以外，我每天都工作。</div><div class="py">Chúle xīngqītiān yǐwài, wǒ měi tiān dōu gōngzuò.</div><div class="id">Except Sundays, I work every day.</div></div>

Trap: 以外 can be dropped (除了他 = 除了他以外). 都/也 stresses "all/also".

## 12.19 一边…一边…

Pattern: **一边 V1, 一边 V2** = while V1-ing, V2-ing (two simultaneous actions).

<div class="contoh"><div class="hz">不要一边吃饭，一边说话。</div><div class="py">Búyào yìbiān chīfàn, yìbiān shuōhuà.</div><div class="id">Don't talk while eating.</div></div>

<div class="contoh"><div class="hz">他一边听音乐，一边做作业。</div><div class="py">Tā yìbiān tīng yīnyuè, yìbiān zuò zuòyè.</div><div class="id">He listens to music while doing homework.</div></div>

Trap: the two actions must be logically doable together. "一边睡觉，一边跑步" is impossible.

## 12.20 先…再…

Pattern: **先 V1, 再 V2** = V1 first, then V2 (sequence).

<div class="contoh"><div class="hz">先做作业，再玩游戏。</div><div class="py">Xiān zuò zuòyè, zài wán yóuxì.</div><div class="id">Do homework first, then play games.</div></div>

<div class="contoh"><div class="hz">你先走，我再走。</div><div class="py">Nǐ xiān zǒu, wǒ zài zǒu.</div><div class="id">You go first, I'll follow.</div></div>

先…再… vs 就: 先…再… = sequence of two actions; 就 = "then/right away" (one action continuing).

## 12.21 还是 vs 或者

Both mean "or", different arenas:

| | 还是 | 或者 |
|---|---|---|
| Meaning | or (choice) | or (choice) |
| Used in | **questions** | **statements** |
| Example | 你喝茶还是喝咖啡？ | 茶或者咖啡都可以。 |

<div class="contoh"><div class="hz">你喝茶还是喝咖啡？</div><div class="py">Nǐ hē chá háishì hē kāfēi?</div><div class="id">Do you want tea or coffee?</div></div>

<div class="contoh"><div class="hz">茶或者咖啡都可以，我不挑。</div><div class="py">Chá huòzhě kāfēi dōu kěyǐ, wǒ bù tiāo.</div><div class="id">Tea or coffee, either is fine, I'm not picky.</div></div>

Trap: 还是 in questions doesn't need 吗 (wrong: 你喝茶还是喝咖啡吗？).
