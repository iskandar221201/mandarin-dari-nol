# Chapter 12: HSK 3 Grammar

HSK 2 gave you the time machine (过/着/正在) and comparison (比/最). HSK 3 adds the **precision engine**: complements that explain the *result* and *direction* of every action, transformation sentences (把/被), and paired conjunctions for reasoning.

This chapter is split into four pages, 35 patterns:

- [Complements](/en/bab-12-komplemen), §§12.1–12.5: directional complements (V+来/去), result complements, potential (V得/V不), state complements (V+得), 极了.
- [把, 被 & Advanced Comparison](/en/bab-12-ba-bei), §§12.6–12.13: full 把, 被 passive, 把 vs 被 contrast, 越来越, 越…越…, 跟…一样, 比…更/还.
- [Compound Sentences](/en/bab-12-majemuk), §§12.14–12.21: 不但…而且…, 如果…就…, 只要…就…, 只有…才…, 除了…以外, 一边…一边…, 先…再…, 还是 vs 或者.
- [Special Verbs & Adverbs](/en/bab-12-verba-keterangan), §§12.22–12.35: causatives (让/叫/请), 看起来, reduplication, separable verbs, 为了, 一直, 终于, 几乎, 才 vs 就, 刚 vs 刚才, 又…又…, 多+Adj.

> Prerequisite: master [Chapter 8](/en/bab-8-tata-bahasa-hsk2). Patterns already taught in HSK 1–2 (basic 比, 过/着/正在, 把-intro, 因为…所以…) are not retaught here, only referenced.
