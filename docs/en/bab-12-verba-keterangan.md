# Chapter 12 · 12.22–12.35: Special Verbs & Adverbs

Closing Chapter 12: verbs with unique behavior and signature HSK 3 adverbs.

## 12.22 让/叫/请: causatives

Pattern: **让/叫/请 + person + V** = make/ask someone do something.

| Word | Tone | Example |
|---|---|---|
| 让 | casual/neutral | 让我想想 |
| 叫 | order | 老师叫我们安静 |
| 请 | polite | 请他帮忙 |

<div class="contoh"><div class="hz">妈妈让我去买东西。</div><div class="py">Māma ràng wǒ qù mǎi dōngxi.</div><div class="id">Mom told me to go shopping.</div></div>

<div class="contoh"><div class="hz">老师叫我们安静，不要说话。</div><div class="py">Lǎoshī jiào wǒmen ānjìng, búyào shuōhuà.</div><div class="id">The teacher told us to be quiet, no talking.</div></div>

<div class="contoh"><div class="hz">请他帮忙，他一定愿意。</div><div class="py">Qǐng tā bāngmáng, tā yídìng yuànyì.</div><div class="id">Ask him for help, he'll surely agree.</div></div>

Trap: 让 also means "let/allow" (让我走 = let me go). Context decides.

## 12.23 看起来…

Pattern: **看起来 + Adj** = looks … (visual impression).

<div class="contoh"><div class="hz">这家餐厅看起来不错。</div><div class="py">Zhè jiā cāntīng kàn qǐlái búcuò.</div><div class="id">This restaurant looks decent.</div></div>

<div class="contoh"><div class="hz">他看起来很高兴。</div><div class="py">Tā kàn qǐlái hěn gāoxìng.</div><div class="id">He looks very happy.</div></div>

Trap: 看起来 = impression ("seems"), not literally "look upward". Don't translate it word by word.

## 12.24 Verb reduplication: V + V

Pattern: **verb repeated** (看看, 试试, 想想) = try … for a bit (casual tone).

<div class="contoh"><div class="hz">你看看这本书，很有意思。</div><div class="py">Nǐ kànkan zhè běn shū, hěn yǒu yìsi.</div><div class="id">Take a look at this book, it's really interesting.</div></div>

<div class="contoh"><div class="hz">让我想想，明天告诉你。</div><div class="py">Ràng wǒ xiǎngxiang, míngtiān gàosu nǐ.</div><div class="id">Let me think, I'll tell you tomorrow.</div></div>

<div class="contoh"><div class="hz">你试试这个菜，很好吃。</div><div class="py">Nǐ shìshi zhè ge cài, hěn hǎochī.</div><div class="id">Try this dish, it's delicious.</div></div>

Note: similar effect to 一下 (看一下), but reduplication is more casual and friendly.

## 12.25 Adjective reduplication

Pattern: **repeated Adj** (+ 地) = in a … manner (vivid, descriptive).

<div class="contoh"><div class="hz">慢慢走，别着急。</div><div class="py">Mànman zǒu, bié zháojí.</div><div class="id">Walk slowly, don't rush.</div></div>

<div class="contoh"><div class="hz">他高高兴兴地回家了。</div><div class="py">Tā gāogāoxìngxìng de huí jiā le.</div><div class="id">He went home happily.</div></div>

Trap: two-syllable adjectives become AABB (高兴 → 高高兴兴), one-syllable become AA (慢 → 慢慢). Add 地 after for manner adverbials.

## 12.26 离合词: separable verbs

离合词 = verb+object fused together (睡觉, 见面, 结婚), but splittable with 了/numbers/time inserted.

<div class="contoh"><div class="hz">我昨天睡了一个小时觉。</div><div class="py">Wǒ zuótiān shuì le yí ge xiǎoshí jiào.</div><div class="id">Yesterday I slept for an hour.</div></div>

<div class="contoh"><div class="hz">我们明天见面吧。</div><div class="py">Wǒmen míngtiān jiànmiàn ba.</div><div class="id">Let's meet tomorrow.</div></div>

<div class="contoh"><div class="hz">他去年结婚了。</div><div class="py">Tā qùnián jiéhūn le.</div><div class="id">He got married last year.</div></div>

Trap: only complements get inserted (了, duration, count). Never insert another object. Common ones: 睡觉, 见面, 结婚, 生气, 帮忙, 聊天.

## 12.27 为了…

Pattern: **为了 + goal** = for the sake of / in order to … (a goal *not yet* realized).

<div class="contoh"><div class="hz">为了学习中文，他每天早起。</div><div class="py">Wèile xuéxí zhōngwén, tā měi tiān zǎoqǐ.</div><div class="id">To learn Mandarin, he gets up early every day.</div></div>

<div class="contoh"><div class="hz">他为了工作，搬到北京了。</div><div class="py">Tā wèile gōngzuò, bān dào Běijīng le.</div><div class="id">For work, he moved to Beijing.</div></div>

为了 vs 因为: 为了 = purpose (forward-looking); 因为 = reason (already happened).

## 12.28 一直…

Pattern: **一直 + predicate** = continuously … (from before until now).

<div class="contoh"><div class="hz">我一直在这家公司工作。</div><div class="py">Wǒ yìzhí zài zhè jiā gōngsī gōngzuò.</div><div class="id">I've been working at this company all along.</div></div>

<div class="contoh"><div class="hz">他一直很忙，没时间见面。</div><div class="py">Tā yìzhí hěn máng, méi shíjiān jiànmiàn.</div><div class="id">He's been busy all the time, no chance to meet.</div></div>

## 12.29 终于…了

Pattern: **终于…了** = finally… (after waiting/effort).

<div class="contoh"><div class="hz">你终于到了！</div><div class="py">Nǐ zhōngyú dào le!</div><div class="id">You finally arrived!</div></div>

<div class="contoh"><div class="hz">我们终于完成了工作。</div><div class="py">Wǒmen zhōngyú wánchéng le gōngzuò.</div><div class="id">We finally finished the work.</div></div>

Trap: 终于 always pairs with 了 and carries a relieved/satisfied tone.

## 12.30 几乎…

Pattern: **几乎 + predicate** = almost … (nearly happened).

<div class="contoh"><div class="hz">他的头发几乎全白了。</div><div class="py">Tā de tóufa jīhū quán bái le.</div><div class="id">His hair has almost all turned white.</div></div>

<div class="contoh"><div class="hz">我几乎忘记了这件事。</div><div class="py">Wǒ jīhū wàngjì le zhè jiàn shì.</div><div class="id">I almost forgot about this.</div></div>

## 12.31 才 (two flavors)

才 has two meanings that look contradictory:

1. **Late/below expectation**: 他二十五岁才上大学。(Only started college at 25, late!)
2. **Merely a little**: 才两个人，别买太多。(Just two people, don't buy too much.)

<div class="contoh"><div class="hz">他二十五岁才上大学。</div><div class="py">Tā èrshíwǔ suì cái shàng dàxué.</div><div class="id">He didn't start college until age 25.</div></div>

<div class="contoh"><div class="hz">才两个人，别买太多菜。</div><div class="py">Cái liǎng ge rén, bié mǎi tài duō cài.</div><div class="id">It's just two people, don't buy too many groceries.</div></div>

## 12.32 才 vs 就

The eternal pair, pulling opposite directions:

| 才 | 就 |
|---|---|
| slow/little (below expectation) | fast/much (above expectation) |
| 他三十岁才结婚 | 他二十岁就结婚了 |

<div class="contoh"><div class="hz">我等了一个小时才到，他十分钟就到了。</div><div class="py">Wǒ děng le yí ge xiǎoshí cái dào, tā shí fēnzhōng jiù dào le.</div><div class="id">I waited an hour before arriving; he arrived in ten minutes.</div></div>

The point: the same number can be "slow" (才) or "fast" (就) depending on the speaker's expectation.

## 12.33 刚 vs 刚才

| 刚 | 刚才 |
|---|---|
| "just now" (attached to the verb) | "a moment ago" (standalone time word) |
| 我刚回来。 | 你刚才去哪儿了？ |

<div class="contoh"><div class="hz">我刚回来，还没吃饭。</div><div class="py">Wǒ gāng huílái, hái méi chīfàn.</div><div class="id">I just got back, haven't eaten yet.</div></div>

<div class="contoh"><div class="hz">你刚才去哪儿了？我找你半天。</div><div class="py">Nǐ gāngcái qù nǎr le? Wǒ zhǎo nǐ bàntiān.</div><div class="id">Where were you just now? I've been looking for ages.</div></div>

Trap: 刚 can't stand alone as a sentence-initial time word (wrong: 刚我回来了).

## 12.34 又…又…

Pattern: **又 + Adj1 + 又 + Adj2** = both … and … (two parallel, usually positive, traits).

<div class="contoh"><div class="hz">这个菜又便宜又好吃。</div><div class="py">Zhè ge cài yòu piányi yòu hǎochī.</div><div class="id">This dish is cheap and tasty.</div></div>

<div class="contoh"><div class="hz">她又聪明又努力。</div><div class="py">Tā yòu cōngming yòu nǔlì.</div><div class="id">She's smart and hardworking.</div></div>

Trap: 又…又… is for two *aligned* traits (usually positive). Use other patterns for negatives.

## 12.35 多么…！

Pattern: **多么 + Adj** = how …! (exclamation of admiration).

<div class="contoh"><div class="hz">多么好！我们完成了！</div><div class="py">Duōme hǎo! Wǒmen wánchéng le!</div><div class="id">How wonderful! We're done!</div></div>

<div class="contoh"><div class="hz">外面多么舒服，我们去公园吧。</div><div class="py">Wàimiàn duōme shūfu, wǒmen qù gōngyuán ba.</div><div class="id">How comfortable it is outside, let's go to the park.</div></div>

Trap: 多么 (how!, exclamation) vs 多少 (how many, question). Totally different.
