# Chapter 13 · Dialogues 4–6

## Dialogue 4: Shopping + complaint

<div class="contoh">
<div class="hz">A: 这件衣服多少钱？</div>
<div class="py">Zhè jiàn yīfu duōshao qián?</div>
<div>How much is this piece of clothing?</div>
</div>

<div class="contoh">
<div class="hz">B: 三百块，很便宜的。</div>
<div class="py">Sān bǎi kuài, hěn piányi de.</div>
<div>Three hundred, very cheap.</div>
</div>

<div class="contoh">
<div class="hz">A: 三百？太贵了！别的地方更便宜。</div>
<div class="py">Sān bǎi? Tài guì le! Bié de dìfang gèng piányi.</div>
<div>Three hundred? Too expensive! Other places are cheaper.</div>
</div>

<div class="contoh">
<div class="hz">B: 你穿上试试，我们的衣服都很好。</div>
<div class="py">Nǐ chuān shang shìshi, wǒmen de yīfu dōu hěn hǎo.</div>
<div>Try it on, all our clothes are good.</div>
</div>

<div class="contoh">
<div class="hz">A: 好吧，我买了。如果有问题，可以退吗？</div>
<div class="py">Hǎo ba, wǒ mǎi le. Rúguǒ yǒu wèntǐ, kěyǐ tuì ma?</div>
<div>Fine, I'll take it. If there's a problem, can I return it?</div>
</div>

<div class="contoh">
<div class="hz">B: 可以。七天以内把衣服拿回来，我们给你换新的。</div>
<div class="py">Kěyǐ. Qī tiān yǐnèi bǎ yīfu ná huílai, wǒmen gěi nǐ huàn xīn de.</div>
<div>Sure. Bring the clothes back within seven days, we'll exchange them for new ones.</div>
</div>

<div class="contoh">
<div class="hz">A: 好，谢谢！</div>
<div class="py">Hǎo, xièxie!</div>
<div>OK, thanks!</div>
</div>

**Dialogue breakdown:**

- **更便宜** *gèng piányi* ("even cheaper"): 比…更… = even more… (advanced comparison, Chapter 12 pattern).
- **穿上** *chuān shang* ("put on"): V + 上 = result complement "attached/on" (Chapter 12 pattern).
- **试试** *shìshi* ("try a little"): verb reduplication = try briefly (Chapter 12 pattern).
- **如果** *rúguǒ* ("if"): 如果…就… for conditions (Chapter 12 pattern).
- **退** *tuì* ("to return"): 退货 = return goods.
- **以内** *yǐnèi* ("within"): 七天以内 = within seven days.
- **把衣服拿回来** *bǎ yīfu ná huílai* ("bring the clothes back"): 把 + object + V + directional complement (full Chapter 12 pattern).
- **换** *huàn* ("to exchange"): 换新的 = exchange for new ones.

<div class="kenapa">
<div class="kenapa-title">🧩 Why this pattern?</div>
<p>Haggling in China is <strong>a sport, not a war</strong>. "Too expensive! Other places are cheaper" (太贵了！别的地方更便宜) is a standard line, not an insult. Sellers actually like it because it means you're serious about buying. And notice: a good complaint always <strong>states clear terms</strong> (如果有问题…): Chinese people respect explicit rules, which is why the seller immediately answers with a concrete return policy (七天以内).</p>
<p><strong>Same pattern:</strong> 把 + complement also appears in Dialogue 2 (把旧的修好).</p>
</div>

## Dialogue 5: Phone call + making plans

<div class="contoh">
<div class="hz">A: 喂，你好！我是小王。星期六你有时间吗？</div>
<div class="py">Wéi, nǐ hǎo! Wǒ shì Xiǎo Wáng. Xīngqī liù nǐ yǒu shíjiān ma?</div>
<div>Hello! I'm Xiao Wang. Are you free on Saturday?</div>
</div>

<div class="contoh">
<div class="hz">B: 星期六？我看看。如果我不工作，就有时间。</div>
<div class="py">Xīngqī liù? Wǒ kànkan. Rúguǒ wǒ bù gōngzuò, jiù yǒu shíjiān.</div>
<div>Saturday? Let me check. If I'm not working, I'll be free.</div>
</div>

<div class="contoh">
<div class="hz">A: 那我们去公园吧。只要天气好，我们就去。</div>
<div class="py">Nà wǒmen qù gōngyuán ba. Zhǐyào tiānqì hǎo, wǒmen jiù qù.</div>
<div>Then let's go to the park. As long as the weather's good, we'll go.</div>
</div>

<div class="contoh">
<div class="hz">B: 好。但是我星期六上午才有时间，下午有会议。</div>
<div class="py">Hǎo. Dànshì wǒ xīngqī liù shàngwǔ cái yǒu shíjiān, xiàwǔ yǒu huìyì.</div>
<div>OK. But I'm only free Saturday morning, there's a meeting in the afternoon.</div>
</div>

<div class="contoh">
<div class="hz">A: 没问题，我们上午十点见。</div>
<div class="py">Méi wèntǐ, wǒmen shàngwǔ shí diǎn jiàn.</div>
<div>No problem, see you at ten in the morning.</div>
</div>

<div class="contoh">
<div class="hz">B: 十点？太早了！十点半吧。</div>
<div class="py">Shí diǎn? Tài zǎo le! Shí diǎn bàn ba.</div>
<div>Ten? Too early! How about half past ten.</div>
</div>

<div class="contoh">
<div class="hz">A: 好，十点半见！</div>
<div class="py">Hǎo, shí diǎn bàn jiàn!</div>
<div>OK, see you at half past ten!</div>
</div>

**Dialogue breakdown:**

- 💡 **Bonus name**: 小王 (Xiǎo Wáng) = "Little Wang", a friendly way to address someone named Wang.
- **看看** *kànkan* ("take a look"): reduplication = do it briefly.
- **如果…就…** *rúguǒ…jiù…* ("if…then…"): ordinary condition (Chapter 12 pattern).
- **只要…就…** *zhǐyào…jiù…* ("as long as…then…"): minimal condition, looser than 如果 (Chapter 12 pattern).
- **才** *cái* ("only…"): 上午才有时间 = *only* free in the morning (emphasis on lateness/scarcity, Chapter 12 pattern).
- **公园** *gōngyuán* ("park"): 公 = public, 园 = garden.
- **会议** *huìyì* ("meeting"): more formal than 开会.

<div class="kenapa">
<div class="kenapa-title">🧩 Why this pattern?</div>
<p>Making plans by phone in China is <strong>a two-way negotiation</strong>, not an announcement. Watch the rhythm: A proposes → B sets a condition (如果) → A loosens it (只要) → B sets a limit (才) → A haggles over time → deal. Nobody says "OK" to the first offer; haggling over timing is polite, not flaky. If you agree instantly without negotiating, the other person may suspect you're not serious.</p>
<p><strong>Same pattern:</strong> 如果…就… also appears in Dialogue 4 (如果有问题).</p>
</div>

## Dialogue 6: Venting + advice

<div class="contoh">
<div class="hz">A: 你怎么了？看起来不高兴。</div>
<div class="py">Nǐ zěnme le? Kàn qǐlai bù gāoxìng.</div>
<div>What's wrong? You look unhappy.</div>
</div>

<div class="contoh">
<div class="hz">B: 最近的事越来越多，我越来越累。</div>
<div class="py">Zuìjìn de shì yuè lái yuè duō, wǒ yuè lái yuè lèi.</div>
<div>Lately there's more and more stuff, I'm getting more and more tired.</div>
</div>

<div class="contoh">
<div class="hz">A: 别担心，跟我说说吧。</div>
<div class="py">Bié dānxīn, gēn wǒ shuōshuo ba.</div>
<div>Don't worry, tell me about it.</div>
</div>

<div class="contoh">
<div class="hz">B: 老板不但给我很多工作，而且周末也让我去公司。</div>
<div class="py">Lǎobǎn búdàn gěi wǒ hěn duō gōngzuò, érqiě zhōumò yě ràng wǒ qù gōngsī.</div>
<div>The boss not only gives me tons of work, but also makes me come in on weekends.</div>
</div>

<div class="contoh">
<div class="hz">A: 你应该跟老板说说，或者找个新工作。</div>
<div class="py">Nǐ yīnggāi gēn lǎobǎn shuōshuo, huòzhě zhǎo ge xīn gōngzuò.</div>
<div>You should talk to the boss, or find a new job.</div>
</div>

<div class="contoh">
<div class="hz">B: 可是我不敢。我担心找不到更好的。</div>
<div class="py">Kěshì wǒ bù gǎn. Wǒ dānxīn zhǎo bú dào gèng hǎo de.</div>
<div>But I don't dare. I'm afraid I can't find anything better.</div>
</div>

<div class="contoh">
<div class="hz">A: 相信自己！你很聪明，一定能找到。</div>
<div class="py">Xiāngxìn zìjǐ! Nǐ hěn cōngming, yídìng néng zhǎodào.</div>
<div>Believe in yourself! You're smart, you'll definitely find one.</div>
</div>

<div class="contoh">
<div class="hz">B: 谢谢你。跟你聊天以后，我觉得好多了。</div>
<div class="py">Xièxie nǐ. Gēn nǐ liáotiān yǐhòu, wǒ juéde hǎo duō le.</div>
<div>Thank you. After chatting with you, I feel so much better.</div>
</div>

**Dialogue breakdown:**

- **看起来** *kàn qǐlai* ("looks…"): V + 起来 = impression from appearance (Chapter 12 pattern).
- **越来越累** *yuè lái yuè lèi* ("increasingly tired"): 越来越 + Adj (Chapter 12 pattern).
- **说说** *shuōshuo* ("chat a bit"): reduplication = casual, informal.
- **不但…而且…** *búdàn…érqiě…* ("not only…but also…"): two piling facts (Chapter 12 pattern).
- **让** *ràng* ("to make/let"): 让 + person + V = causative (Chapter 12 pattern).
- **或者** *huòzhě* ("or"): for stated options (vs 还是 for questions).
- **敢** *gǎn* ("to dare"): 不敢 = don't dare.
- **找不﻿到** *zhǎo bú dào* ("can't find"): V + 不 + 到 = negative potential (Chapter 12 pattern).
- **相信** *xiāngxìn* ("to believe"): 相信自己 = believe in yourself.
- **聊天** *liáotiān* ("to chat"): 天 = day, "chat until you lose track of time".

<div class="kenapa">
<div class="kenapa-title">🧩 Why this pattern?</div>
<p>Venting in Chinese culture is <strong>a two-step ritual</strong>: vent first (说说吧), advise later. Jumping straight to advice without listening first is considered rude. That's why A opens with "don't worry, tell me" before daring to say "you should…". And good advice always <strong>offers two options</strong> (或者): talk to the boss OR find a new job. One option sounds like an order; two options sound like a friend.</p>
<p><strong>Same pattern:</strong> 不但…而且… also works for positive things, e.g. "she's not only smart but also hardworking".</p>
</div>
