# Chapter 14: HSK 3 Mock Exam

A 28-question mock exam to measure your readiness before taking the real HSK 3. Do it seriously, don't peek at the vocabulary list, and note your score at the end.

## What the real exam looks like

The real HSK 3 has **80 questions**: 40 listening (±35 min), 30 reading (±30 min), 10 writing (±15 min). Total **300 points**, pass at **180**. From HSK 3 on, the scale is 300, not 200 like HSK 1–2.

This web version is the adaptation: **28 questions, no audio**. We turned the listening part into reading questions. One important thing: in the real HSK 3, pinyin in the reading section is drastically reduced. That's why in this mock, **some reading questions have no pinyin at all**, just hanzi. Pure hanzi reading practice, like the real thing.

To be honest, this is no substitute for the real exam, but it's enough to tell whether you're ready.

## How to score yourself

Out of 28 questions, **24 count toward your score** (Sections A through D). The four Warm-up questions are unscored.

Your formula: **score divided by 24, times 300**. Example: 18 correct means 18 / 24 x 300 = 225. The real exam pass mark: **180**, which means at least **15 correct answers**.

## Tips

- Set aside about 35 minutes with no distractions.
- Work in order from Warm-up through Section D.
- Section C has no pinyin on purpose. Don't panic, guess from context.
- Section D (word ordering) is closest to the real writing section.
