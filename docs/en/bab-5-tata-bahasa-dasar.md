# Chapter 5: Basic Grammar

This chapter summarizes the 19 HSK 1 grammar patterns — the foundation for every sentence you'll build. Because this chapter is long, the material is split into three pages:

- [Chapter 5 · 1–7: Basic Sentences](/en/bab-5-kalimat-dasar) — S-V-O structure, 是, 吗, 呢, 很, 太...了, 也/都
- [Chapter 5 · 8–13: Modifiers & Adverbials](/en/bab-5-penjelas-keterangan) — demonstratives, measure words, 的, 和, question words, time expressions
- [Chapter 5 · 14–19: Existence & Position](/en/bab-5-keberadaan-posisi) — 在, 有, 不/没, modal verbs, 了, position words + exercises

> **Reading note:** pinyin in this book is written in **base tone** (dictionary form). When speaking, apply the tone-change rules from Chapter 0 — e.g. 不 *bù* is read *bú* before a 4th tone (不是), 你好 *nǐ hǎo* is read *ní hǎo*.
