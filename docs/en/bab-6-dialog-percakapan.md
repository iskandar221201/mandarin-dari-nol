# Chapter 6: Dialogues & Conversation

Theory without practice is like owning a bike you never ride. This final chapter has 10 short dialogues from everyday situations. Read each line **out loud** (not just in your head), then study the word breakdowns. The dialogues are split into two pages:

- [Chapter 6 · Dialogues 1–5](/en/bab-6-dialog-1-5) — introductions, age & origin, restaurant, shopping, directions & time
- [Chapter 6 · Dialogues 6–10](/en/bab-6-dialog-6-10) — phone calls, school, weather, inviting someone to eat, goodbyes + exercises
