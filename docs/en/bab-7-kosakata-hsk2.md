# Chapter 7: HSK 2 Vocabulary

Welcome to HSK 2. At this level your vocabulary grows by **±150 new words** (total ±300 words). The words get more specific: extended family, everyday objects, food, adjectives, and activity verbs.

This chapter is split into three pages:

- [People & Things](/en/bab-7-orang-benda), §§7.1–7.5: extended family, new pronouns, home & objects, public places, body.
- [Food & Adjectives](/en/bab-7-makanan-sifat), §§7.6–7.8: new food and HSK 2 adjectives.
- [Activities](/en/bab-7-kegiatan), §§7.9–7.11: routines, hobbies & sports, interaction verbs.

> How to use: every new word comes with example sentences. Don't memorize the tables raw; read the examples and absorb the patterns.
