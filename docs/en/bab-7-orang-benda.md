# Chapter 7 · 7.1–7.5: People & Things

## 7.1 Extended family

In HSK 1 you learned 爸爸, 妈妈. HSK 2 completes the family tree:

| Hanzi | Pinyin | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 哥哥 | gēge | older brother |
| 姐姐 | jiějie | older sister |
| 弟弟 | dìdi | younger brother |
| 妹妹 | mèimei | younger sister |
| 丈夫 | zhàngfu | husband |
| 妻子 | qīzi | wife |
| 孩子 | háizi | child |
| 男人 | nánrén | man |
| 女人 | nǚrén | woman |
| 姓 | xìng | family name; to be surnamed |

<div class="contoh"><div class="hz">我有两个哥哥。</div><div class="py">Wǒ yǒu liǎng gè gēge.</div><div class="id">I have two older brothers.</div></div>

<div class="contoh"><div class="hz">她是我的妻子。</div><div class="py">Tā shì wǒ de qīzi.</div><div class="id">She is my wife.</div></div>

<div class="contoh"><div class="hz">你贵姓？</div><div class="py">Nǐ guì xìng?</div><div class="id">What is your family name? (polite)</div></div>

> 💡 贵 (guì, "expensive/noble") here is the polite form of 姓. Details in §7.7.

<div class="bedah"><strong>姓</strong> (xìng, family name) = 女 (woman) + 生 (to be born). Cultural trace: family names were inherited through the mother's line in ancient China.</div>

Note: 男人/女人 = 男/女 + 人, a neat pattern. 丈夫/妻子 must be memorized as a pair.

## 7.2 New pronouns & markers

| Hanzi | Pinyin | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 您 | nín | you (polite) |
| 它 | tā | it (objects/animals) |
| 大家 | dàjiā | everyone |
| 每 | měi | every |

<div class="contoh"><div class="hz">您好！欢迎！</div><div class="py">Nín hǎo! Huānyíng!</div><div class="id">Hello (polite)! Welcome!</div></div>

<div class="contoh"><div class="hz">大家都喜欢他。</div><div class="py">Dàjiā dōu xǐhuan tā.</div><div class="id">Everyone likes him.</div></div>

<div class="contoh"><div class="hz">每天我都跑步。</div><div class="py">Měi tiān wǒ dōu pǎobù.</div><div class="id">I run every day.</div></div>

Key pattern: **每 + noun + 都**. 每 (every) almost always pairs with 都 (all): 每天都, 每个人都.

The tā trio is complete: 他 (he) · 她 (she) · 它 (it). Read identically, written differently.

## 7.3 Home & objects

| Hanzi | Pinyin | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 房间 | fángjiān | room |
| 门 | mén | door |
| 手机 | shǒujī | mobile phone |
| 手表 | shǒubiǎo | watch |
| 报纸 | bàozhǐ | newspaper |
| 铅笔 | qiānbǐ | pencil |
| 票 | piào | ticket |
| 颜色 | yánsè | color |

<div class="contoh"><div class="hz">我的房间很大。</div><div class="py">Wǒ de fángjiān hěn dà.</div><div class="id">My room is big.</div></div>

<div class="contoh"><div class="hz">这个手机很贵。</div><div class="py">Zhè ge shǒujī hěn guì.</div><div class="id">This phone is expensive.</div></div>

<div class="bedah"><strong>手机</strong> (shǒujī, mobile phone) = 手 (hand) + 机 (machine) → "hand machine". <strong>手表</strong> (shǒubiǎo) = 手 + 表 (display) → "hand display" = watch.</div>

## 7.4 Public places & directions

| Hanzi | Pinyin | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 机场 | jīchǎng | airport |
| 火车站 | huǒchēzhàn | train station |
| 宾馆 | bīnguǎn | hotel |
| 教室 | jiàoshì | classroom |
| 公司 | gōngsī | company |
| 路 | lù | road |
| 公共汽车 | gōnggòngqìchē | bus |
| 左边 | zuǒbian | left side |
| 右边 | yòubian | right side |
| 外 | wài | outside |
| 旁边 | pángbiān | beside |
| 服务员 | fúwùyuán | waiter/attendant |

<div class="contoh"><div class="hz">机场在左边。</div><div class="py">Jīchǎng zài zuǒbian.</div><div class="id">The airport is on the left.</div></div>

> 💡 **Bonus word** (outside HSK 2, but essential at restaurants): 买单 (mǎidān) = ask for the bill / pay.

<div class="contoh"><div class="hz">服务员，买单！</div><div class="py">Fúwùyuán, mǎidān!</div><div class="id">Waiter, the bill please!</div></div>

<div class="bedah"><strong>机场</strong> (jīchǎng, airport) = 机 (machine/plane) + 场 (field) → "plane field". <strong>火车站</strong> = 火车 (train) + 站 (station).</div>
