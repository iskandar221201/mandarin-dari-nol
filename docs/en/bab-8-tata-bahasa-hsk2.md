# Chapter 8: HSK 2 Grammar

HSK 1 gave you sentence foundations (是, 有, 在). HSK 2 adds Mandarin's **time and comparison machines**: how to talk about past experiences, ongoing states, comparisons, and compound sentences.

This chapter is split into three pages:

- [Aspect & Time](/en/bab-8-aspek), §§8.1–8.4: 过 (experience), 着 (durative), 正在, 已经, 再, 就.
- [Comparison & Degree](/en/bab-8-perbandingan), §§8.5–8.7: 比, 最, 非常/真, 得 (complement), 地.
- [Combined Sentences](/en/bab-8-gabungan), §§8.8–8.11: 因为/虽然, 从/往/离, 吧/别, intro to 把, big numbers.

> Prerequisite: master [Chapter 5](/en/bab-5-tata-bahasa-dasar) first. Every pattern here builds on Chapter 5.
