# Hanzi Pattern Gallery

*A bank of the most common patterns. Memorize the patterns, not the hanzi.*

This page is the "periodic table" of the [Hanzi Dissection Method](/en/metode-bedah-hanzi): a collection of component patterns that repeat across thousands of hanzi. Once a pattern is in your head, new hanzi just need matching.

## 1. Sound families: one phonetic component, many hanzi

The most profitable pattern: **one right/bottom component gives the same sound to many hanzi** — just swap the left radical to change the meaning.

### The 马 (mǎ, horse) family → sound "ma"

| Hanzi | Pinyin | Meaning | Dissection |
|---|---|---|---|
| 妈 | mā | mother | 女 (woman) + 马 (sound) |
| 吗 | ma | question particle | 口 (speech) + 马 (sound) |

The pattern: the right component 马 always contributes the sound "ma". What decides the meaning is the left radical: 女 (woman) → mother; 口 (mouth/speech) → a question particle that's spoken. Once you get this pattern, every hanzi with 马 on the right gives away its sound immediately.

### The 也 (yě) family → sound "ye/ta"

| Hanzi | Pinyin | Meaning | Dissection |
|---|---|---|---|
| 他 | tā | he/him | 亻 (person) + 也 (sound) |
| 她 | tā | she/her | 女 (woman) + 也 (sound) |
| 地 | dì / de | earth / particle | 土 (earth) + 也 (sound) |

The pattern: 也 is a "sound machine" for ta/ye. Notice 他 and 她: read exactly the same (tā), differing only in radical → differing gender in writing.

### The 青 (qīng, blue/green) family → sound "qing"

| Hanzi | Pinyin | Meaning | Dissection |
|---|---|---|---|
| 请 | qǐng | please; to ask | 讠 (speech) + 青 (sound) |
| 清 | qīng | clear | 氵 (water) + 青 (sound) |
| 情 | qíng | feeling | 忄 (heart) + 青 (sound) |
| 晴 | qíng | clear (weather) | 日 (sun) + 青 (sound) |

The most beautiful pattern: the sound "qing" is consistent across all members while the radicals swap roles. 请 = "speech that sounds like qing" → to ask (with speech). 清 = "water that sounds like qing" → clear. See this pattern once and you'll never confuse 请/清/情/晴 again.

### The 生 (shēng, to be born) family → sound "sheng/xing"

| Hanzi | Pinyin | Meaning | Dissection |
|---|---|---|---|
| 星 | xīng | star | 日 (sky body) + 生 (sound) |
| 姓 | xìng | family name | 女 (woman) + 生 (sound) |

## 2. Meaning families: one radical, many hanzi

The reverse: **one radical gives the same meaning category** to many hanzi.

### 氵 (water) → think water/liquid

汉 (Hàn, in 汉语 — the Han River), 酒 (jiǔ, alcohol), 洗 (xǐ, to wash). See 氵 on the left → first guess "this is about water/liquid", usually right.

### 扌 (hand) → think hand actions

打 (dǎ, to hit/to call), 找 (zhǎo, to look for), 把 (bǎ, to hold). See 扌 → "this is a hand action".

### 口 (mouth) → think mouth/sound/speech

叫 (jiào, to call), 吃 (chī, to eat), 喝 (hē, to drink), 吗 (ma, question particle). See 口 → "this is about the mouth or sound".

## 3. Shape variants: radicals in disguise

Radicals often slim down when squeezed to the left of a hanzi. These six are the most common — memorize the pairs:

| Full radical | Variant | Position | Examples |
|---|---|---|---|
| 人 rén (person) | 亻 | left | 你, 他 |
| 心 xīn (heart) | 忄 | left | 想, 情 |
| 手 shǒu (hand) | 扌 | left | 打, 找 |
| 水 shuǐ (water) | 氵 | left | 汉, 清 |
| 言 yán (speech) | 讠 | left | 说, 请 |
| 食 shí (food) | 饣 | left | 饭 |

The rule is simple: **slim shape = left position**. When you see 亻, 忄, 扌, 氵, 讠, or 饣, immediately recognize them as left-side variants of their full radicals.

## 4. How to use this gallery

Don't memorize it all at once. Every time a [dissection](/en/metode-bedah-hanzi) turns up a familiar component ("hey, that's 马 again"), open this page and match it to its family. Gradually the patterns stick on their own — and every pattern that sticks saves dozens of hanzi from rote memorization.
