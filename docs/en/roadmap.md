# Roadmap: After HSK 2

This book doesn't stop at HSK 2. This page is the official roadmap, what's finished, what's being worked on, and what features are planned for this site.

## Current status: zero → HSK 2 ✅

HSK 1 and HSK 2 coverage is done and ready to study right now:

- **HSK 1 (7 chapters)**: Chapter 0 Setup (pinyin & tones) · Chapter 1 Greetings & Introductions · Chapter 2 Numbers & Time · Chapter 3 Radicals & Hanzi Breakdown · Chapter 4 Everyday Vocabulary · Chapter 5 Basic Grammar · Chapter 6 Dialogues & Conversation
- **HSK 2 (3 chapters)**: Chapter 7 HSK 2 Vocabulary (people, things, food, adjectives, activities) · Chapter 8 HSK 2 Grammar (aspect, comparison, combined sentences) · Chapter 9 HSK 2 Dialogues (phone, office, restaurant, travel, shopping, doctor)
- **~300 core words** + **~30 grammar patterns** + interactive quizzes in every chapter
- Every example only uses vocabulary from the HSK 1–2 lists, no "smuggled-in" words you haven't been introduced to yet

## Coming: HSK 3 📋

- **~300 additional words** (total ~600 words)
- Compound sentences: "because... therefore...", "although... but...", "the more... the more..."
- Full 把 / 被 + more natural passive patterns
- Short readings and simple paragraph-writing practice

## Planned site features

- **Pronunciation audio** on every example sentence (click to listen)
- **Interactive quizzes** per chapter, not just static answer keys
- **Hanzi handwriting practice mode** with stroke order
- **Vocabulary search** across the whole book

> **The HSK 2+ pages are COMING SOON, contributions via GitHub are open.** If you spot a mistake on any HSK 1 page, or want to help write the HSK 2 chapters, click "Edit this page on GitHub" on any page.
