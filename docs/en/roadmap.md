# Roadmap: After HSK 1

This book doesn't stop at HSK 1. This page is the official roadmap — what's finished, what's being worked on, and what features are planned for this site.

## Current status: zero → HSK 1 ✅

HSK 1 coverage is done and ready to study right now:

- **7 chapters**: Chapter 0 Setup (pinyin & tones) · Chapter 1 Greetings & Introductions · Chapter 2 Numbers & Time · Chapter 3 Radicals & Hanzi Breakdown · Chapter 4 Everyday Vocabulary · Chapter 5 Basic Grammar · Chapter 6 Dialogues & Conversation
- **~150 core words** + **18 grammar patterns** + a 60-question exercise bank
- Every example only uses vocabulary from the HSK 1 list — no "smuggled-in" words you haven't been introduced to yet

## Coming: HSK 2 🚧

Next coverage target:

- **~150 additional words** (total ~300 words)
- **Past & future sentence patterns**: 了 for completed events, 过 for experience ("ever"), 会 for "will"
- **Introducing 把 & 被**: the two most distinctively Mandarin sentence patterns
- Richer conjunctions and adverbs (because, so, if, already/not yet)
- New situational dialogues: at the office, traveling, more formal phone calls

## Coming: HSK 3 📋

- **~300 additional words** (total ~600 words)
- Compound sentences: "because... therefore...", "although... but...", "the more... the more..."
- Full 把 / 被 + more natural passive patterns
- Short readings and simple paragraph-writing practice

## Planned site features

- **Pronunciation audio** on every example sentence (click to listen)
- **Interactive quizzes** per chapter — not just static answer keys
- **Hanzi handwriting practice mode** with stroke order
- **Vocabulary search** across the whole book

> **The HSK 2+ pages are COMING SOON — contributions via GitHub are open.** If you spot a mistake on any HSK 1 page, or want to help write the HSK 2 chapters, click "Edit this page on GitHub" on any page.
