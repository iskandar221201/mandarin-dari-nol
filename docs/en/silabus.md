# Syllabus

This page explains which standard "Mandarin from Zero" follows, so you know which official exam our material maps to. In short: we follow **HSK 2.0**, the standard used by HSK levels 1 through 6.

## The standard we follow

HSK (汉语水平考试, Hànyǔ Shuǐpíng Kǎoshì) is the internationally recognized Chinese proficiency test. We follow **HSK 2.0**, launched in 2010 by **Hanban** (the Chinese Language Office). The exam is currently run by **Chinese Testing International**, and official info lives at [chinesetest.cn](https://www.chinesetest.cn).

Official HSK 2.0 vocabulary counts (new words per level and cumulative total):

| Level | New words | Cumulative total |
|---|---|---|
| HSK 1 | 150 | 150 |
| HSK 2 | 150 | 300 |
| HSK 3 | 300 | 600 |
| HSK 4 | 600 | 1,200 |
| HSK 5 | 1,300 | 2,500 |
| HSK 6 | 2,500 | 5,000 |

**This site covers HSK 1 (150 words) + HSK 2 (150 new words, 300 total).** Finish every chapter here and your vocabulary matches the HSK 2 scope.

## The real exam format

So you know what the actual target looks like, here is the real HSK exam format:

**HSK 1**

- 40 questions: 20 listening + 20 reading
- 200 points total, pass at 120

**HSK 2**

- 35 listening + 25 reading + 10 writing questions
- 200 points total, pass at 120

An honest note: audio for the listening section is not available on this site yet, so our listening practice is simulated through text. You can still drill the question patterns, but for real listening practice, use audio from official sources or other apps.

## About HSK 3.0

There is a new standard: **HSK 3.0**. China's Ministry of Education published it in 2021 (9 levels in 3 bands), then **revised the exam syllabus in November 2025, effective July 2026**. Its vocabulary numbers differ a lot from the 2021 version, so ignore the numbers in older articles. These are the current figures (cumulative):

| Level | Vocabulary | Grammar points |
|---|---|---|
| HSK 1 | 300 | 70 |
| HSK 2 | 500 | 148 |
| HSK 3 | 1,000 | 244 |
| HSK 4 | 2,000 | 339 |
| HSK 5 | 3,600 | 409 |
| HSK 6 | 5,400 | 459 |
| HSK 7-9 | 11,000 | 593 |

This site does **not** follow HSK 3.0 yet. The reason is simple: current exams still run on HSK 2.0, and we want to wait until the new syllabus is actually stable in use before evaluating a migration. Development plans live on the [Roadmap](/en/roadmap) page.

The official HSK 3.0 syllabus is on [CTI's syllabus page](https://www.chinesetest.cn/syllabus).

## Sources

We separate two kinds of sources so you know what is primary and what is secondary:

**Primary (official)**

- [chinesetest.cn](https://www.chinesetest.cn): the official HSK organizer's site, for exam info, schedules, and syllabi.
- CTI's official syllabi: 《新汉语水平考试大纲》 for HSK 2.0 (print/paid) and the 2025 《HSK考试大纲》 for HSK 3.0.
- [HSK Standard Course](https://www.blcup.com) (《HSK标准教程》): the official Hanban-licensed series from Beijing Language and Culture University Press and CTI. Book 1 covers 150 words and 45 language points for HSK 1.

**Secondary (reference)**

- [mandarinbean.com](https://mandarinbean.com): a widely circulated HSK 2.0 word list. **To be honest: our vocabulary lists here are based on secondary copies like this one, cross-checked against the HSK Standard Course. They are not an "official list" from the exam organizer.**
- [Chinese Grammar Wiki](https://resources.allsetlearning.com/gramwiki/?title=HSK_1_grammar_points) (AllSet Learning): a transparent, per-level list of HSK grammar points.

We do not redistribute copyrighted material (printed syllabi, books, question banks). For the original documents, visit the official links above.
