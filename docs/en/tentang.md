# About This Book

## Who this book is for

For **absolute beginners** who want to learn Mandarin from zero — no assumption that you've ever studied Mandarin before. It's a good fit for:

- self-learners who need a clear structure (where to start, where to go next);
- anyone taking an offline course who needs companion material for practicing at home;
- anyone who's just curious and wants to try the first 7 chapters with zero commitment.

You don't need to be able to read Chinese characters first. Chapter 0 teaches you pinyin and tones from scratch.

## How to use it

1. **Follow the chapters in order, 0 → 6.** Each chapter builds on the previous one — don't skip ahead.
2. **Read the examples out loud**, not just in your head. Mandarin is a tonal language; your mouth needs the workout, not just your eyes.
3. **Do the exercises at the end of each chapter before moving on.** If your exercise score is below 70%, repeat the chapter once more — no shame, nobody's grading you but you.
4. **Memorize vocabulary gradually** following the chapter order (see the HSK 1 Vocabulary List page). A realistic target: 10–15 new words per day.
5. Finished HSK 1? Check the [Roadmap](/en/roadmap) page for the path onward to HSK 2.

## About the author

Written by **Asep Iskandar** — who **started learning Mandarin from zero in October 2026 and wrote this book while learning**.

That's not just a polite *disclaimer*: it means the explanations in this book are written from the perspective of someone who just went through the exact same confusion you're going through. If an explanation is wrong or confusing, there's a good chance it's the part the author himself was still figuring out — and precisely because of that, every page has a correction button.

Found a typo, a wrong pinyin, or a misleading explanation? Don't let it slide:

1. Click the **"Edit this page on GitHub"** link on every page.
2. Edit directly in the browser, then submit as a *pull request*.
3. Or open a new *issue* if you're not sure what the fix should be — discussion is also contribution.

All corrections are verified before being merged. Thanks for helping keep this book's quality up.
