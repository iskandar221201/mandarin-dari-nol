# HSK 1 Mock Exam

A web-based simulation of the HSK 1 exam. If you have read all the HSK 1 chapters (Chapter 0 through 6) and worked through the exercise bank, this is your moment of truth.

**The real format:** the actual HSK 1 exam has 40 questions (20 listening + 20 reading), 200 points total, you pass at 120, and it takes about 40 minutes.

**The adaptation on this page (being honest first):** since there is no audio on the web yet, the listening part is simulated through text. So there are 24 questions in total: 4 warm-up questions (unscored, to check your readiness) + 20 scored questions focused on reading and writing.

**How to play:**

- Work through the questions in order, no peeking at the answers first.
- Aim for about 25 minutes. You can use your phone timer.
- Your final score = the number of correct answers out of the 20 scored questions.
- Convert to the 200-point scale: **score × 10**. You pass at **12 or more** (equivalent to 120).

| Correct answers | 200 scale | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 16–20 | 160–200 | Solid pass. Your HSK 1 foundation is done, ready for HSK 2. |
| 12–15 | 120–150 | Pass. But strengthen your dialogues (Chapter 6) first before leveling up. |
| 0–11 | 0–110 | Not yet. Repeat Chapters 1–5, redo the exercise bank, then try again next week. |

A score is not a verdict. If you did not pass, you now know exactly which part has holes, and that is progress too. Check the [roadmap](/en/roadmap) for your next steps.
